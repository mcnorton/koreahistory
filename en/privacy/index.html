Privacy Policy

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Korean policy, the Korean version applies.

This privacy policy applies to the 한국사연표 mobile app (the "Application"), created by McNorton Edu (the "Service Provider") as an ad-supported service. The service is provided "as is".

1. Information collection and use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (for example, IP address)
  • The pages of the Application you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system of your mobile device

The Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Service Provider may use the information you provide to contact you from time to time with important information, required notices, and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, the Service Provider may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information while using the Application. Information requested by the Service Provider is retained by the Service Provider and used as described in this privacy policy.

2. Third-party access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically sent to external services to help the Service Provider improve the Application and its service. The Service Provider may share your information with third parties in the ways described in this privacy policy.

The Application uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies on data handling. Links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store: https://www.apple.com/kr/privacy

The Service Provider may disclose information you provide and information collected automatically:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process
  • When it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect its rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request

We work with trusted service providers who act on our behalf, do not independently use the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to follow the rules in this privacy policy.

3. Your right to opt out

You can stop all collection of information easily by deleting the Application. You can use the standard deletion process built into your mobile device, or the process offered through the mobile app marketplace or network.

4. Data retention policy

The Service Provider retains user-provided data while you use the Application and for a reasonable period afterward. To delete user-provided data you gave through the Application, contact mcnorton.dev@gmail.com and you will receive a reply within a reasonable time.

5. Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under 13.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If the Service Provider discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal information, it will delete that information from its servers immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and know that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact the Service Provider at mcnorton.dev@gmail.com so that necessary action can be taken.

6. Security

The Service Provider is committed to keeping your information confidential, and provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information it processes and maintains.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of changes by updating this page with the new policy. Please check this policy regularly for changes. Continued use is considered acceptance of all changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of June 12, 2025.

8. Your consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of your information as set out in this privacy policy, now and as amended by us.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about privacy while using the Application, or about our privacy practices, contact the Service Provider by email at mcnorton.dev@gmail.com.

10. Website feedback form

This section applies to the feedback form on the website and takes effect on November 4, 2026.

  • Information received: category, your message, and a nickname (optional). We do not ask for your name or email address, so please don't enter personal information in your message or nickname.
  • Purpose: fixing errors, improving the app and website, and writing release notes.
  • Delivery and storage: what you send is delivered to the Service Provider's Slack channel and stored there. It is not stored separately on the website. During delivery, the external services that handle transmission and storage may process connection information under their own privacy policies.
  • Nickname: your nickname may appear publicly in the release notes (Version History). Leave it blank if you prefer not to be named.
  • Deletion: to delete a message or nickname you sent, email mcnorton.dev@gmail.com with the date and content.
