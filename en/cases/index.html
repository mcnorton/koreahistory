Use Cases

Here are some ways to use the app. If you have a better idea or a story from your own classroom, please tell us.

Ideas for schools outside Korea

These are suggestions to adapt for your students. Students need a device with the app, and most lessons work best with a teacher or a translation app to help with the Korean text.

Korean history

Timeline, with world history on

  • Pick an event students know, such as World War II, and find what was happening in Korea that year.
  • Make a two-column timeline of Korea and your own country for one period.
  • Follow the Korean independence movement by bookmarking events, then present them to the class.

Hangul and Korean language

Search and Timeline

  • Search for 한글 and 조선어, then put the events in order to tell the story of the alphabet.
  • Practice reading: find years, era names, and familiar names in real Korean sentences.
  • Older learners can translate a short event description, then check it against the linked article.

Korean culture

Home

  • Use the home screen to talk about this month's Korean national holidays and why they matter.
  • Compare today's solar and lunar dates, then discuss Korean holidays that follow the lunar calendar, such as Seollal and Chuseok.

Family history

My History

  • Students interview relatives and record dated family events in their own language.
  • Students place family events beside Korean and world events from the same years.

Classroom use in Korea

These ideas were written for elementary classes in Korea, and many of them adapt well to other settings.

Grade 2: family history book project

My History

  • Research project on students' own and their families' history
  • Presentation project on modern history, using screen sharing

Grade 3: history

Timeline, prehistoric eras

  • Individual study of how prehistoric periods are divided
  • Research on how civilizations developed in each era
  • Individual comparison of world and Korean history

Grade 5: history

Timeline and Wikipedia details

  • Detailed research on the founding of states and major events in each era
  • Individual study of the flow of time up to modern history
  • Project on relations with neighboring countries, analyzing causes and effects alongside world history
  • Individual study of the history of war, diplomacy, and cultural exchange
  • Research on the people's efforts for independence

Grade 6: modern history

Timeline eras and Wikipedia

  • Research on the founding of the government after liberation and the efforts of independence activists
  • Study of how modern government and democracy took shape
  • Project on the founding of the republic, democratization movements, and their causes and effects
  • Research on recent events and government efforts toward reunification

My History and Timeline bookmarks

  • Research the history of democratization movements and present findings
  • Bookmark independence movement events and stage a role play
  • Bookmark historical figures and draw a comic about one person's story

Research background

Studies in Korean on teaching with timelines:

  • 박희진, 역사 연표를 활용한 지속과 변화의 개념학습 방안, 경인교육대학교 교육대학원, 2018

    • A lesson on continuity and change using students' "life history timelines"
    • Could be applied to making timelines by topic

  • 이창온, 역사수업에서 연표학습 활용방안 : 고등학교 세계사 일본 근대사의 이해, 이화여자대학교 교육대학원, 2013

    • Recommends timeline study as a method suited to history, for developing historical time concepts, awareness, and thinking
    • Presents timeline activities for high school world history

  • 김영찬, 디자인역사연구방법론 연구와 이를 기반으로 한 3D디자인역사연표 개발 : 형태와 행위 구조 개념을 기반으로, 한양대학교 대학원, 2011

    • The concept, structure, and design meaning of three-dimensional timelines by topic
    • Timeline ideas for K-12 topics such as people, science, art, and war
