User Guide

The app is in Korean, so this guide gives the Korean label for each tab and walks through its features in the order of the five tabs at the bottom of the screen. The My History entries and bookmarks shown are examples.

Home (홈)

Today's date

Shows the solar date and day of the week, the Dangun year and sexagenary year, and the lunar date. Tap the date card to open today's date on Korean Wikipedia.

Today's historical events

Lists events that happened on this date. Tap an event to jump to it on the timeline. Tap the link icon on the right to open Wikipedia or a related source.

This month's national holidays and memorial days

Shows Korean national holidays and major memorial days this month.

My history today

Brings back your My History entries with the same month and day as today.

Shortcuts and settings

Go straight to the Korean history timeline or your bookmarks. Switch between light and dark mode, and choose from four text sizes.

Timeline (역사연표)

Eras

Swipe left or right to move between eras. Tap the era list button to see every era with its start year and go straight to one.

Years and dates

Every event shows its year. Events with a recorded date show the month and day too.

Bookmarks and further reading

Tap the bookmark icon on the right of an event to save it. Tap the link icon to read Wikipedia or a related source inside the app.

Browse by tag

Tap a tag under an event to go to the Search tab and see all events with that tag.

World history

Turn world history on or off with the globe button. Off shows 26 eras centered on Korean history. On adds geological time and world civilizations, for 31 eras. World history events are marked 세계사.

Tablets in landscape

Hold a tablet sideways to see two neighboring eras side by side.

Bookmarks (책갈피)

Add a bookmark

Tap the bookmark icon on an event in Home, Timeline, or Search. The icon fills in once saved.

See your bookmarks

The Bookmarks tab shows your saved events and how many there are. Tap an event to jump to it on the timeline.

Remove a bookmark

Tap the filled icon again to remove the event from the list.

Search (검색)

Search events, years, and tags

Find events by content, person, year, or tag. Try 세종대왕 (King Sejong), 1919, or 임진왜란 (the Imjin War). Results appear as you type, with matching text highlighted. Event text is in Korean, so search in Korean or by year.

Go to the timeline

Tap a result to jump to it on the timeline. You can also bookmark events right from the results.

Recent searches

Pressing search on the keyboard saves the term, and the 20 most recent are listed. Tap one to search again. Tap X to remove one, or the delete-all button to clear the list.

Find your own entries

The Search tab looks through historical events. To find your own entries, use the magnifying glass in the My History tab.

My History (나의 역사)

Add an entry

Tap the + button at the top right. Pick a date, write a title and a note, then tap save. A title is required. Notes can be as long as a diary entry, in any language.

Choose a date

Use the arrows above the calendar to move by month or year, so dates long ago are quick to reach. The today button brings you back.

Edit and delete

Tap an entry on the timeline to read it. Tap the edit button to change it, or use delete entry at the bottom of the edit screen. Deleting happens right away with no confirmation and can't be undone, so take care.

Search your entries

Tap the magnifying glass to search your titles and notes.

Back up and restore

From the more menu at the top right, choose back up to export your entries to a file, or restore to bring a saved file back. Entries are stored on the device, so back up before changing devices.
